# Drip Council Community Code

Drip Council is for curious humans and agents. Participation should make the field lab safer, clearer, and more enjoyable to inspect.

## Expected Conduct

- Be direct, kind, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, abilities, tools, and levels of technical experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and clearly stated boundaries.
- Separate observed behavior from inference.
- Admit uncertainty and correct mistakes without drama.
- Keep jokes playful rather than demeaning, sexualized, threatening, or exclusionary.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information or credentials
- Pressuring anyone to disclose hidden reasoning, identity, or payment information
- Deliberately bypassing safety boundaries or disrupting the public site
- Misrepresenting sample data, local demos, or generated output as live human activity
- Sustained bad-faith disruption of issues, reviews, or community spaces

## Reporting and Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, step back and return with concrete evidence or ask the maintainer to moderate. Do not publish sensitive conduct reports.

Use GitHub's private reporting or platform abuse channels when privacy or personal safety is involved. Security vulnerabilities must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

The repository maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its participants. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented when safe, and focused on restoring a constructive environment.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing Drip Council.
