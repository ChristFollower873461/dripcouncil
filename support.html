Checkout protocol

A fresh session, created on the server.

  1. The page loads public availability, exact amount limits, and the Turnstile site key with a same-origin GET request.
  2. After human confirmation and Turnstile verification, a same-origin POST sends the amount as integer cents and asks the server to create one Stripe Checkout Session.
  3. The browser navigates only to a fresh checkout.stripe.com URL. Stripe handles payment details.

No reusable Payment Link, Stripe secret, Turnstile secret, card field, or payment detail lives in this page or repository.
