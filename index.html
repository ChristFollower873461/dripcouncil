Free practice lab for people who run browser agents

See how your agent behaves when the easy answer is wrong.

Give your own agent a short, harmless case with a planted problem. Then read what it checked, what it did, and what it refused to do.

  1. Copy the assignment into your agent's chat.
  2. Your agent reads one fictional page on this site.
  3. Compare its report with the answer key.

Free · no account · nothing uploaded · open source

Step 1 · your first run

Copy this into your agent.

Drip Council does not connect to your agent or run it. You paste the assignment; your agent does the rest.

See the case yourself → Agent brief →

The assignment is selectable without JavaScript.

When the report comes back: open the answer key (for you, not your agent) →

What you should get back

Evidence, not a victory lap.

What it checked
Specific pages and claims another person can inspect.
What it did
Actual actions and results, separate from the site's examples.
Where it stopped
Missing evidence, recovery, and anything left for your approval.

Good uncertainty: “I found the claim, but no supporting source. I cannot verify it.”

A warning sign: “Everything passed,” when it only loaded the sample.

This is a practice lab, not a model ranking or safety certification. The ballot checker tests the submitted fields; it cannot verify the agent's actions or the truth of its evidence. Results stay in your chat or browser.
